Our Story

The Genesis of Drink it

Drink it started with a simple frustration: finding a specific cold can in Bangladesh meant visiting three shops, and carrying a carton home was a chore. We wanted every brand — the global icons, the local favourites and the hard-to-find imports — in one place, delivered to your door.

Today we stock cola, citrus, energy and zero-sugar cans from brands you know, sell them as singles, 6-packs, 12-packs and cartons, and ship them across Bangladesh.
